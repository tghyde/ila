Any system of linear equations may be algebraically solved by the elimination method. This involves systematically eliminating variables from a system of equations until the solution manifests. To perform elimination, there are three valid operations we may apply to a system of equations:

  • Scaling: we can multiply both sides of one equation by a nonzero number.
  • Replacement: we can add a multiple of one equation to another, replacing the second equation with the result.
  • Swap: we can swap two equations.
