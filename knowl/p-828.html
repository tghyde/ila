The proofs of the multiplicativity property and the transpose property below, as well as the cofactor expansion theorem in Section 4.2 and the determinants and volumes theorem in Section 4.3, use the following strategy: define another function and prove that satisfies the same four defining properties as the determinant. By the existence theorem, the function is equal to the determinant. This is an advantage of defining a function via its properties: in order to prove it is equal to another function, one only has to check the defining properties.
