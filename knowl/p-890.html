Let us review what we actually proved in Section 4.1. We showed that if is any function satisfying the four defining properties of the determinant (or the three alternative defining properties), then it also satisfies all of the wonderful properties proved in that section. In particular, since can be computed using row reduction by this recipe in Section 4.1, it is uniquely characterized by the defining properties. What we did not prove was the existence of such a function, since we did not know that two different row reduction procedures would always compute the same answer.
