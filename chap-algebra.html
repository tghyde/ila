Chapter1Systems of Linear Equations: Algebra¶ permalink

Primary Goal

Solve a system of linear equations algebraically in parametric form.

In this chapter we study systems of linear equations and their solutions algebraically. We will learn a systematic way to solve systems of equations of the form

In Section 1.1, we introduce systems of linear equations, the heart of linear algebra. In Section 1.2, we present a procedure, called row reduction, for finding all solutions of a system of linear equations. In Section 1.3, we show how to express all solutions of a system of linear equations using the parametric form of the general solution.
