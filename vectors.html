Recall that an element is called a point. Geometrically we may think of a point as a little dot floating in space. There is a useful alternative perspective given by thinking of an element of as an arrow extending from the origin to the point With this in mind we call a vector.

Points and Vectors

A vector is a point in drawn as an arrow.

The difference is purely psychological: points and vectors are both just lists of numbers.
